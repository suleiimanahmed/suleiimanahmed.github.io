Selected Projects

Ongoing — PhD research

THM Modelling of Permafrost Degradation

Coupled thermo-hydro-mechanical simulation of permafrost in OpenGeoSys, focused on how degradation mechanisms affect ground stability and infrastructure performance, including a field-scale case study of pore-water salinity effects at Norman Wells, Northwest Territories.

OpenGeoSys · C++ · Field calibration data

Completed — Oct 2025

Loyola Campus Geothermal Feasibility Study

Field investigation supporting a geothermal feasibility assessment for a low-carbon district heating and cooling network at Concordia University's Loyola Campus, under the PLAN/NET ZERØ initiative with industry partner Énergir. Contributed to subsurface characterization through exploratory drilling and sample collection.

Field investigation · Subsurface sampling

Completed — 2024

Seismic Response of Stone-Column-Improved Soils

M.Eng. thesis project analyzing the effect of stone columns (gravel piles) on the seismic response of soft soils, for a proposed Science and Technology Park in Thessaloniki, Greece. Used 1D non-linear site response analysis under Eurocode-8-based input motions.

DEEPSOIL · Equivalent-linear & effective-stress analysis
