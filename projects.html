Coupled thermo-hydro-mechanical simulation in OpenGeoSys, focused on degradation mechanisms, ground stability and infrastructure performance. The field-scale case study considers pore-water salinity effects at Norman Wells, Northwest Territories.

MethodsOpenGeoSys · C++ · field calibration data
