About

Engineering across research, fieldwork and teaching.

PhD student in Civil Engineering (Geotechnical) at Concordia University, in the Energy and Environmental Geomechanics Group under Dr. Biao Li.

My research applies thermo-hydro-mechanical numerical modelling to permafrost degradation, ground stability and cold-region infrastructure.

I hold an M.Eng. in Civil Engineering from the Democritus University of Thrace, Greece, where my thesis examined the influence of stone columns on seismic soil response. Before returning to research, I worked on construction projects in Greece and Kenya and volunteered with student and community organisations.

I follow how AI tools can support modelling, scripting and analysis. Verification, engineering judgement and responsibility stay with the engineer.
