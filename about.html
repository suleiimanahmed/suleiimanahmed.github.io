About

Background

Geotechnical engineer and PhD student applying finite element and multiphysics methods to cold-region ground problems, with prior field and site-engineering experience in Greece and Kenya.

Biography

Suleiman Ahmed is a PhD student in Civil Engineering (Geotechnical Engineering) at Concordia University, working within the Energy and Environmental Geomechanics Group under Dr. Biao Li. His research applies thermo-hydro-mechanical (THM) numerical modelling to permafrost degradation and its consequences for ground stability and infrastructure performance in cold regions, using the open-source multiphysics platform OpenGeoSys.

He holds an M.Eng. in Civil Engineering from the Democritus University of Thrace, Greece, where his thesis examined the effect of stone columns on the seismic response of soils, supervised by Dr. Emmanouil Rovithis. Before returning to research, he worked as a site and civil engineer on construction projects in Greece and Kenya, and has volunteered extensively with student and community organizations in both countries.
